Sweet Shelf

A triple-match sort in a bakery display. Drag a treat to another shelf; three of a kind sell out. Every shelf hides a second row, and clearing the front brings it forward, so one good move often sets up the next.

Genre
Triple-match sort
Input
Drag and drop, or tap and tap
Length
About 25 s, 40 s timer
Largest build
55.9 KB
  • The hand drags the first match; idle players get a hint, and TikTok builds play on their own
  • Combo callouts and a "SOLD OUT!" finish, or a "So close!" card when time runs out
  • The hint looks two moves ahead, so auto-play always reaches the win

Laundry Sort

Tap clothes to send them to the washing machine of the same color. Wrong color goes to the basket; fill the basket and the load is lost. At the end a red sock sneaks into the white machine and dyes everything pink.

Genre
Sorting puzzle
Input
Tap
Length
15–30 s, then end card
Largest build
61.8 KB
  • A hand shows the first move; the basket warns before it fills up
  • A short twist at the end: the pink load is the moment people remember
