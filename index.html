Laundry Sort

Tap clothes to send them to the washing machine of the same color. Wrong color goes to the basket; fill the basket and the load is lost. At the end a red sock sneaks into the white machine and dyes everything pink.

Inside the playable

  • A hand shows the first move; idle players get hints, and TikTok builds advance on their own
  • The call to action appears only after the player has touched the game
  • Sound stays off until the first tap and stops when the ad isn't on screen
  • Art is drawn in code, so nothing loads from the network
  • Top and bottom edges stay clear for each network's close and skip controls

Working together

  1. Send your assetsSprites, UI, sounds, store links and a gameplay video. No assets yet? Simple custom art is possible.
  2. Agree on a storyboardThe hook, the core interaction and the end card, planned before any code.
  3. Get the first build fastA playable for your main network, then revisions and builds for the rest.
Every build is a single package with no runtime network requests, checked against the network's current specification before delivery.
